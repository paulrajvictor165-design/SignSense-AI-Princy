1. Problem Statement

"Over 285 million people worldwide are visually impaired, including 39 million who are completely blind. They face daily challenges navigating surroundings, reading text, identifying currency, and accessing emergency services — without adequate AI-powered assistive tools."

Existing accessibility solutions are:

❌ Expensive

Commercial visual assistants cost hundreds of dollars per year, pricing out users in developing nations like India.

❌ Limited Scope

Most tools handle only one task (e.g., text reading or navigation) rather than a unified real-time assistant.

❌ Poor Offline Support

Cloud-only tools fail in low-connectivity rural areas where accessibility needs are greatest.

❌ Language Barriers

Most assistants support English only, ignoring users of Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, and regional languages.

2. Our Solution — SignSense AI

SignSense AI is a free, open-source, AI-powered mobile accessibility assistant that continuously analyzes the user's surroundings through the smartphone camera and delivers real-time voice guidance — acting like a human companion.

  • 1
    Real-Time Object Detection
    Continuously detects 80+ objects: chairs, stairs, doors, people, animals and announces position ("Chair on your left")
    YOLOv8n · OpenCV · Camera stream
  • 2
    Smart Navigation Assistant
    Turn-by-turn walking directions with live GPS tracking and OpenStreetMap routing
    OpenRouteService · Nominatim · flutter_map
  • 3
    Traffic Signal Detection
    Detects red/yellow/green signals and pedestrian crossings with voice alerts
    YOLOv8 + HSV color analysis
  • 4
    OCR Text Reader
    Reads books, medicine labels, street boards, restaurant menus, newspapers in English and Hindi
    EasyOCR · Multi-language support
  • 5
    AI Scene Description (Gemini)
    Full natural-language environment narration: "You are near a busy road. Two people are walking ahead."
    Google Gemini 1.5 Flash (Free Tier)
  • 6
    Indian Currency Detection
    Identifies ₹10, ₹20, ₹50, ₹100, ₹200, ₹500, ₹2000 notes by denomination
    Gemini Vision · Custom YOLOv8 model
  • 7
    Emergency SOS
    One-tap SOS sends live GPS location via SMS to emergency contact + direct dial to 112
    Geolocator · SMS · url_launcher
  • 8
    Voice Command Interface
    Completely hands-free: "Detect objects", "Navigate", "Read text", "SOS" — zero tapping required
    flutter_tts · speech_to_text
  • 9
    Vehicle Detection
    Detects approaching cars, bikes, buses, trucks and warns with direction ("Vehicle approaching from left")
    YOLOv8 COCO classes
  • 10
    Face Detection
    Detects number and position of people's faces — helps user know if someone is nearby
    MediaPipe Face Detection
  • 11
    Color Detection
    Identifies dominant colors in the scene — helps with clothing selection and object identification
    OpenCV HSV analysis · 12 color classes
  • 12
    Offline Mode
    Core detection features (YOLO, OCR, colors) work without internet using on-device models
    On-device models · sqflite · No cloud dependency

5. Accessibility Standards

🎙️ Voice-First Design

Every screen announces itself when opened. All buttons have semantic labels for screen readers. No visual-only feedback.

👁️ Visual Adaptations

Dark mode by default. High-contrast mode. Text scale 80%–200%. Bounding boxes with high-contrast corner markers.

🔇 Haptic Feedback

Optional vibration for key alerts (SOS sent, obstacle detected). Reduces reliance on visual or audio alone.

🌐 Multi-Language

Voice output in English (India), Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada. OCR supports English and Hindi scripts.

10. Conclusion

SignSense AI demonstrates that world-class AI assistive technology does not require expensive hardware or paid services. By combining YOLOv8, Google Gemini, OpenStreetMap, and Flutter — all free and open-source — we deliver a production-quality accessibility assistant that can transform the independence and quality of life for millions of visually impaired individuals worldwide.
